## Common Refund Questions

### How long does a refund take?

3 days for UPI, 7 days for card, and 10 days for Cash on Delivery orders
(paid by bank transfer). See `refund-payout-timelines.md`.

### Why is my refund taking longer than that?

Refunds above ₹20,000 need a human agent to approve them before the
timeline above starts. See `refund-approval-thresholds.md`.

### I paid Cash on Delivery, how do I get refunded?

By bank transfer — ShopEase collects your account details after the
return or replacement is approved. See `cod-refund-process.md`.

### My order was expensive, does that change anything?

Orders above ₹50,000 get extra handling as high-value orders, which is
separate from the ₹20,000 refund-approval threshold. See
`high-value-order-policy.md`.
