## Defect Replacement Window

If a product arrives faulty, or develops a manufacturing defect shortly
after delivery, the customer can claim a **replacement within 15 days**
of delivery.

### How This Differs From a Standard Return

- This window applies **regardless of the product's standard return
  window** — including categories like air conditioners, which have no
  standard return window at all (see `returns-acs.md`).
- The replacement is for a defective unit, not a change of mind; ShopEase
  may ask for photos or a short description of the defect.

### After 15 Days

Past the 15-day defect window, the issue is handled as a warranty claim —
see `warranty-overview.md` and `warranty-acs.md`.
