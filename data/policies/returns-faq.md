## Common Return Questions

### How many days do I have to return my product?

It depends on the category: 7 days for phones, 10 days for TVs and most
other categories, 15 days for mixers, and air conditioners cannot be
returned for a change of mind. See `returns-overview.md` for the full
table.

### My product is broken, does the return window apply?

No — a defective product can be replaced within 15 days of delivery
regardless of the standard return window. See
`defect-replacement-policy.md`.

### Can I return an item after the window has passed?

Not through a standard return. If it's defective, see
`defect-replacement-policy.md`; if it's still within warranty, see
`warranty-overview.md`.

### Do I need the original packaging?

Yes, for a standard return. See the category-specific policy
(`returns-phones.md`, `returns-tvs.md`, `returns-mixers.md`,
`returns-other-categories.md`) for exact conditions.
