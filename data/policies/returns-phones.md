## Return Window

Phones can be returned within **7 days** of delivery.

### Conditions

- The device must be unused, factory-reset, and in its original box with
  all accessories.
- Screen protectors and cases applied by the customer do not affect
  eligibility.
- Phones returned after 7 days are not eligible for a standard return; see
  `defect-replacement-policy.md` if the issue is a defect.

## Refund

Once a phone return is received and inspected, the refund follows the
timeline in `refund-payout-timelines.md`.
