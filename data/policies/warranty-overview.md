## Standard Warranty

Most ShopEase products carry a manufacturer warranty of **12 months**
from the date of delivery, covering manufacturing defects under normal
use.

### What's Covered

- Manufacturing defects and premature component failure.
- Not covered: physical damage, liquid damage, unauthorized repairs, or
  normal wear and tear.

### Exceptions

- Air conditioners carry a longer, 24-month warranty — see
  `warranty-acs.md`.

### Making a Claim

Within the first 15 days of delivery, a defective product is handled as a
replacement — see `defect-replacement-policy.md`. After that, it is
handled as a warranty repair/replacement claim through the manufacturer's
process referenced on the invoice.
