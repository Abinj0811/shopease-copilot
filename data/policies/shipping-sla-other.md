## Delivery Time Outside Metro Cities

Orders shipping outside the six metro cities (see `shipping-sla-metro.md`)
are expected to arrive within **7 days** of being placed.

### Why It's Longer

Non-metro deliveries route through more regional logistics hops, so
ShopEase quotes a longer SLA to avoid setting expectations it can't meet.

### If Delivery Is Late

If an order hasn't arrived within the 7-day window, treat it as delayed —
this is a common trigger for a `delivery_delay` support ticket.
