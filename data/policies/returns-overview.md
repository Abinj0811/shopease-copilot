## How Returns Work

ShopEase accepts returns on most products within a fixed number of days of
delivery, counted from the delivery date. The exact window depends on the
product category — see the category-specific policy for the number that
applies to your item.

| Category | Return window |
|---|---|
| Phones | 7 days |
| TVs | 10 days |
| Air conditioners | Not eligible for standard return |
| Mixers | 15 days |
| Laptops, refrigerators, washing machines, headphones | 10 days |

### Eligibility Conditions

To qualify for a standard return, the item must be:

- Requested within its category's return window (see table above)
- Unused and in its original packaging
- Not damaged through misuse

### Not a Standard Return?

- If the product is faulty or defective, see `defect-replacement-policy.md`
  — the replacement window is separate from the standard return window.
- Air conditioners are not covered by standard returns; see
  `returns-acs.md` and `warranty-acs.md`.
