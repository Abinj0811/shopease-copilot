## Standard Returns Are Not Available

Air conditioners have a **0-day** standard return window — once an AC
order is delivered (and, in most cases, installed), it cannot be returned
simply because the customer changed their mind.

### If the Unit Is Faulty

A faulty or defective AC can still be claimed as a replacement under
`defect-replacement-policy.md`, which applies regardless of the standard
return window.

### Warranty

ACs carry an extended manufacturer warranty — see `warranty-acs.md`.
