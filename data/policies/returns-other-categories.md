## Return Window

Laptops, refrigerators, washing machines, and headphones follow ShopEase's
**default 10-day** return window, measured from the delivery date.

### Conditions

- **Laptops:** must be reset to factory settings, with no personal data,
  and include the charger and original box.
- **Refrigerators / washing machines:** must be uninstalled and in
  working, undamaged condition; ShopEase does not provide uninstallation.
- **Headphones:** ear tips/cushions and all accessories must be included;
  hygiene items (ear tips) affect eligibility if opened and used.

## Refund

Once the return is received and inspected, the refund follows the
timeline in `refund-payout-timelines.md`.
