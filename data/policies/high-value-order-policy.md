## What Counts as High-Value

Any order with a total above **₹50,000** is treated as high-value.

### Extra Handling

- High-value orders get extra verification during processing (address and
  payment checks) before shipping.
- If a high-value order needs a refund, the refund amount itself is
  checked separately against the ₹20,000 human-approval threshold in
  `refund-approval-thresholds.md` — most high-value order refunds will
  cross that threshold and need a human agent.

### Do Not Confuse With Refund Approval

This ₹50,000 figure is about the **order total**, not the refund amount.
An order can be high-value without ever needing a refund, and a refund
can need human approval (₹20,000+) on an order that was not high-value.
