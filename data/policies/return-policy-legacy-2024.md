## Deprecated — Do Not Use

**This document describes ShopEase's 2024 return policy and has been
superseded.** It is kept only for historical reference. For the current
policy, use `returns-overview.md` and its category-specific documents.

### Old Return Windows (No Longer in Effect)

- Phones: 14 days
- TVs: 21 days
- Mixers: 30 days
- Air conditioners: 10 days

These figures do not match ShopEase's current return_window_days
configuration and must not be quoted to customers.

### Old Refund Timeline (No Longer in Effect)

All refunds, regardless of payment method, were paid within 5 days. This
has been replaced by the per-payment-method timeline in
`refund-payout-timelines.md`.
