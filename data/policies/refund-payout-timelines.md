## How Long a Refund Takes

Once a return or replacement is approved, the refund is paid out on the
following timeline, based on the original payment method:

| Payment method | Refund paid within |
|---|---|
| UPI | 3 days |
| Card | 7 days |
| Cash on Delivery (COD) | 10 days (via bank transfer — see `cod-refund-process.md`) |

### Notes

- The timeline starts from the date the refund is **approved**, not the
  date the return is requested.
- Refunds above a certain amount require human approval before this
  timeline starts — see `refund-approval-thresholds.md`.
