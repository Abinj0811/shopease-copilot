## Human Approval Above ₹20,000

Refunds of **more than ₹20,000** require review and approval by a human
agent before they are processed. This applies regardless of payment
method or product category.

### What This Means for Customers

- Smaller refunds are approved automatically once a return or replacement
  is verified, and follow the timelines in `refund-payout-timelines.md`.
- Larger refunds are queued for a human agent; the customer should be told
  their request has been escalated, not given a promised refund amount or
  date.

### Do Not Confuse With High-Value Order Handling

This ₹20,000 threshold is about **refund amount**, not order value. A
separate, higher threshold applies to how high-value *orders* are handled
in general — see `high-value-order-policy.md`.
