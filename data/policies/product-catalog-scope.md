## What ShopEase Sells

ShopEase sells electronics and home appliances in eight categories:
phones, TVs, air conditioners, mixers/mixer-grinders, laptops,
refrigerators, washing machines, and headphones.

### Out of Scope

ShopEase does not sell products outside these categories — for example,
furniture, groceries, clothing, or vehicles (including electric
scooters). If a customer asks about a product outside this list, the
copilot should say it's unsure whether ShopEase carries it and offer to
connect them with a human agent, rather than guessing or inventing a
product.

### No Business-Rule Numbers Apply Here

This document defines catalog scope only. Return windows, refund
timelines, warranty lengths, and shipping SLAs are defined per category
in the other policy documents, not here.
