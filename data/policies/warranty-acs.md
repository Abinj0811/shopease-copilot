## Extended Warranty for ACs

Air conditioners carry an extended manufacturer warranty of **24 months**
from the date of delivery — double the standard 12-month warranty other
categories receive (see `warranty-overview.md`).

### Why ACs Are Different

ACs are not eligible for a standard return (see `returns-acs.md`), so the
longer warranty is the primary protection against defects that surface
after the 15-day replacement window in `defect-replacement-policy.md`.

### What's Covered

Same coverage terms as the standard warranty: manufacturing defects and
premature component failure, not physical/liquid damage or unauthorized
repairs.
