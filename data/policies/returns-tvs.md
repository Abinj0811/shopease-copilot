## Return Window

TVs can be returned within **10 days** of delivery.

### Conditions

- The screen must be free of cracks, dead pixels caused by mishandling, or
  physical damage.
- Original packaging, remote, and stand/mount hardware must be included.
- Wall-mounted TVs must be uninstalled before pickup; ShopEase does not
  provide uninstallation service.

## Refund

Once a TV return is received and inspected, the refund follows the
timeline in `refund-payout-timelines.md`.
