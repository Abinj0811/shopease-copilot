## Return Window

Mixers and mixer-grinders can be returned within **15 days** of delivery —
the longest standard return window ShopEase offers.

### Conditions

- Jars, blades, and lids must be unused and included.
- The unit must not show signs of use (stains, odor, wear on blades).

## Refund

Once a mixer return is received and inspected, the refund follows the
timeline in `refund-payout-timelines.md`.
