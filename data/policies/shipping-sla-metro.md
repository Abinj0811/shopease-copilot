## Delivery Time in Metro Cities

Orders shipping to a metro city are expected to arrive within **3 days**
of being placed.

### Metro Cities

Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru, Chennai, Kolkata, Hyderabad.

### If Delivery Is Late

If an order hasn't arrived within the 3-day window, treat it as delayed —
this is a common trigger for a `delivery_delay` support ticket.
